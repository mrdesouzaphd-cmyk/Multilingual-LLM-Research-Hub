AUDI-GPTedu Routine: Recycling Slide Content via Learning-Objective Audit ChatGPT + Bloom's Taxonomy Applied to Instructional Slide Design

Denise da Vinha Ricieri (first author), Raphaela V. G. Barreto, Fabiano Rodrigues de Souza (third author), Adriana M. G. de Farias  ·  CoBICET 2023 — IV Brazilian Interdisciplinary Congress on Science and Technology, Online, Aug–Sep 2023  ·  Peer-reviewed full paper

Research Question

Can a structured 3-prompt ChatGPT routine reliably audit and realign slide-learning objectives to Bloom's taxonomy — reducing educator workload without sacrificing instructional quality?

Methodology

  • Model: GPT-4 via ChatGPT (April 2023), US-geolocated account.
  • Prompt class: In-Context Learning (ICL) — no fine-tuning required; prompt structure carries all task specification.
  • The AUDI-GPTedu 3-prompt routine:
    • A1 — Topic extraction: prompt generates an itemized topic list from raw slide content.
    • A2 — "Grande Ideia" anchor: prompt isolates the central learning concept as a single anchor phrase.
    • A3 — Bloom's verb alignment: prompt identifies the correct cognitive-level verb (Bloom's taxonomy) for the lesson objective.
  • Test corpus: 3 existing lesson slide files from real classroom contexts.
  • Validation: GPT-4 outputs cross-checked against original lesson plans by the research team.
  • Language control: English prompts used, consistent with multilingual bias findings (Souza et al., CoBICET 2023).

Key Findings

  • 3-prompt routine achieved 100% concordance with original lesson plans across all 3 test files.
  • ICL (no fine-tuning) sufficient for structured audit tasks when prompt design is systematic and explicit.
  • A2 "Grande Ideia" extraction reliably identified the central learning concept across diverse subject areas.
  • A3 Bloom's verb alignment enabled objective-level quality control without requiring expert review at each step.
  • Routine is replicable by educators without machine-learning or programming expertise.

Implications for Practice

The AUDI-GPTedu routine offers a practical, low-barrier workflow for educators to audit and recycle existing slide content — aligning it to Bloom's taxonomy without rebuilding from scratch.

The 3-step decomposition (topic list → anchor phrase → taxonomy verb) mirrors how experienced instructional designers think, making AI assistance transparent and auditable.

Companion paper to the PROMPT-EDU framework (Ricieri et al., SCIAS 2024) and the multilingual bias study (Souza et al., CoBICET 2023).
